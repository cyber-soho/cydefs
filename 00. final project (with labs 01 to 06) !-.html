The estate — five zones, five machines

Everything lives in your own isolated pod on the CyberQuébec range (OpenNebula 6.10, FireEdge Sunstone). VMs are wired through Service Templates — the roles bring the networks with them.

Default-deny between zones — every crossing is an explicit rule you can point to. Reference addressing; adjust inside RFC 1918 if you document why. Quota capped at four VMs? Fold mon-01 onto dc-01 and record the exception in the Manual — documented exceptions are engineering, silent ones are findings.
