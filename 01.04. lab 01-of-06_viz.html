§0 — Stop. Read This Before You Touch a Keyboard.

The difference between a technician and a criminal is not skill. It is authorisation.

✅ What you MAY do
  • Run every supplied script on a machine you personally own, or your own isolated pod on the CyberQuébec range.
  • Inspect, break, rebuild and delete your own virtual machines.
  • Replace any tool with a different one (§7), provided you document what and why.
  • Ask a locally installed AI model for help, under the rule in §0.4.
❌ What you MUST NOT do — no exceptions
  • Scan, probe, log into or "test" any machine that is not yours.
  • Run scripts on an employer's equipment without a signed, dated, scoped authorisation.
  • Upload logs, configs, screenshots, IPs or credentials to any hosted AI service.
  • "Hack back" or investigate a machine that appears to be attacking you.
  • Put any real password, private key or personal information in your archive.

"Your authority ends at your own boundary, and your boundary is defined by a contract — not by a router."

0.3 — The Law You Are Working Under

Unauthorised access to, interference with, or interception of a computer system or its data is a criminal offence.

  • 🇨🇦 Criminal Code of Canada, s. 342.1 & s. 430(1.1)
  • 🇺🇸 CFAA, 18 U.S.C. §1030
  • 🇪🇺 Directive 2013/40/EU on attacks against information systems
  • 🇨🇦 Quebec Law 25 and federal PIPEDA on personal information

Ignorance of scope is not a defence. If you are not certain an action is authorised, then it is not.

§2 — Words You Need First

HostAny single computer on a network.
VMA whole computer that exists as a file. Safe to break and delete.
IP address / subnetA machine's postal address, e.g. 172.16.30.15. Subnet = a street of them.
Port / serviceA numbered door (443, 445, 22) and the program behind it.
0.0.0.0Wildcard: "every network card on this machine." Reachable from guest Wi-Fi.
127.0.0.1 (loopback)"This machine only." Traffic never touches a network card.
FirewallA guard at a doorway, allowing/blocking traffic by written rules.
VLANSplits one physical switch into several separate networks.
ZoneSystems sharing the same trust level and access rules, separated by a control point that inspects traffic between them. ⚠️ Two VLANs with a router and an "allow any" rule are NOT two zones.
Default denyThe last rule blocks everything. Every allowed flow is a decision you can name.
EvidenceA file that proves a claim, e.g. "WAN blocks inbound TCP 3389 after rule 4."

§7 — Everything Here Is Open. Change It. 🔧

Every tool, script, technique, address plan and most exercise content is open and substitutable. Three conditions only:

  1. Achieve the same control outcome.
  2. Record the swap in CHANGE_LOG.md — what and why.
  3. Own it at validation time.

"Substituting is engineering. Substituting silently is how audits fail."
