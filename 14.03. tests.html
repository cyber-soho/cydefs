Cryptography and Secure Communication

This Study Hub gathers the key ideas of Class 14: symmetric and asymmetric encryption, hash functions and steganography, salted password storage, data in transit and at rest, and the attacks that target cryptosystems. Memorize the terms with the flashcards, then test your judgment on realistic NorthMaple situations in the quiz.

Quick tip

Most questions ask you to choose a control for a situation, not to recite a definition. For each one, name the state of the data (at rest, in transit, in use), the security service needed (confidentiality, integrity, authentication, non-repudiation) and the attacker (online or offline). The right answer protects that state, provides that service and stops that attacker.
