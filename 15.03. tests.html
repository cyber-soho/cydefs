Quick pro-tip

A certificate is public: anyone can copy it. What proves a server's identity is a fresh signature made with the matching private key — the CertificateVerify message of TLS (Transport Layer Security) 1.3. When a question mentions a stolen key, think revoke, re-key, re-issue.
